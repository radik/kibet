FONTLOG for Kibet Serif
=======================

Kibet Serif is the brand typeface of Kibet (Berlin). It is a modified
version of PT Serif 1.000W by ParaType Ltd., released under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1. As required by the license, the modified
fonts do not use the Reserved Font Names "PT Sans", "PT Serif" or
"ParaType".

ChangeLog

2026-11-16 (Kibet) Kibet Serif / Kibet Serif Display 1.000
  - Renamed families to "Kibet Serif" and "Kibet Serif Display".
  - Redrawn asterisk (U+002A): the "Kibet star", an eight-petal rosette
    referencing Tatar floral ornament; weight-matched for Regular/Bold,
    slanted for the italics.
  - Kibet Serif Display (Regular, Bold): spacing tightened by 22 units
    per glyph (UPM 1000) for headlines and garment prints.
  - Removed hdmx/LTSH/VDMX tables (recomputed by renderers).

2026-11-16 (Kibet) Kibet Serif Light / Kibet Serif Display Light 1.000
  - New Light weight (usWeightClass 300) derived from the Regular
    outlines: contours offset inward by 12 units (UPM 1000), cap height,
    x-height, ascender and descender restored to the Regular values.
  - Advance widths reduced by 12 units per glyph to keep word images
    compact.
  - Curves converted to fine polylines; TrueType hinting removed.
